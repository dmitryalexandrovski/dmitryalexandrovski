### 📡 01 // DIRECTIVE & EXECUTIVE PROFILE

I operate at the intersection of **deep neural architectures, biophysical signal processing, and high-stakes medical compute**. My work focuses on replacing empirical clinical guesswork with deterministic, high-bandwidth artificial intelligence.

Currently serving as **Co-founder & CTO at [CortexNeo](https://cortexneo.digital/)**, orchestrating end-to-end engineering of our deep-learning clinical decision intelligence engine for hyper-acute ischemic stroke intervention. When cerebral tissue undergoes ischemia, every 60 seconds of delay destroys 1.9 million neurons. We build autonomous volumetric inference systems engineered to shave critical minutes off triage and predict catastrophic post-intervention hemorrhage risks before the first scalpel or catheter moves.

### 🧬 02 // RESEARCH & ENGINEERING TAXONOMY

#### `[01]` Volumetric Computer Vision & Neural Biomarkers
* **High-Dimensional Spatial Tensors**: Processing raw isotropic/anisotropic 3D DICOM & NIfTI volumes with sub-millimeter precision.
* **Tissue Characterization**: Dense feature extraction for ischemic core localization, collateral flow grading, and micro-hemorrhagic transformation forecasting.
* **Explainable AI (XAI)**: High-resolution gradient-weighted class activation mapping (Grad-CAM), 3D volumetric attribution tensors, and counterfactual clinical explanations doctors can audit in real time.

#### `[02]` High-Performance Deep Learning Systems
* **Deterministic Inference Engines**: Optimized execution graphs with ONNX Runtime, TensorRT quantization (FP16/INT8), and custom CUDA kernels for minimal latency overhead.
* **Architectures**: Hybrid 3D Convolutional-Transformer backbones (Swin UNETR, 3D Attention U-Nets) calibrated for ultra-low signal-to-noise biomedical datasets.
* **Statistical Rigor**: Bayesian uncertainty estimation and calibration curves to prevent overconfident boundary predictions in life-or-death scenarios.

#### `[03]` Resilient Infrastructure & Distributed Backends
* **Distributed Services**: Fault-tolerant microservices written in high-efficiency Python (FastAPI/AsyncIO) and TypeScript, built for air-gapped hospital networks and cloud orchestration.
* **Data Pipelines**: High-throughput distributed storage, PACS/VNA DICOM router integrations, Redis cache clusters, and ACID-compliant state engines (PostgreSQL).
* **Enterprise Reliability**: Zero-downtime containerized architectures (Docker, Kubernetes) adhering to strict medical compliance and data anonymization standards.
