> **Hi there 👋**
> I am an engineer and system architect specializing in **high-reliability AI infrastructure for modern healthcare**. 
> 
> My work centers on seamlessly bridging heterogeneous medical data — electronic health records (EHR), medical imaging, genomics, and real-time biometric telemetry — directly into clinical decision-making.
